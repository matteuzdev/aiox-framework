# Strategic Review Workflow

## Overview
Workflow para planejamento estratégico e revisão

## Quando usar
- Planejamento trimestral
- Revisão de estratégia
- Decisões de alto nível

## Stages

### Stage 1: Intelligence (konig-research-intel-core)
**Duração:** 5-7 dias

**Steps:**
1. Deep market research
2. Competitive analysis
3. Trend identification
4. Opportunity mapping

**Entrega:** Market intelligence report

---

### Stage 2: Strategy (konig-athenaeum-core)
**Duração:** 7-14 dias

**Steps:**
1. Synthesize intelligence
2. Generate strategic scenarios
3. Assess human & cultural factors
4. Build strategic narrative
5. Present to Council

**Entrega:** Strategic brief aprovado

---

### Stage 3: Governance (konig-governance-core)
**Duração:** 2-3 dias

**Steps:**
1. Audit company health
2. Review metrics
3. Identify risks
4. Recommend actions

**Entrega:** Health report

---

### Stage 4: Context (konig-context-core)
**Duração:** 1-2 dias

**Steps:**
1. Consolidar contexto
2. Update briefing
3. Sync all teams

**Entrega:** Consolidated context

---

### Stage 5: Planning (konig-planning-core)
**Duração:** 5-7 dias

**Steps:**
1. Translate strategy to plan
2. Prioritize initiatives
3. Resource allocation
4. Create roadmap

**Entrega:** Quarterly plan

---

## Dependencies
- konig-research-intel-core → konig-athenaeum-core
- konig-athenaeum-core → konig-governance-core
- konig-governance-core → konig-context-core
- konig-context-core → konig-planning-core

## Owner
**Stage Lead:** Chief Strategist
**Executive Sponsor:** CAIO + Conselho
